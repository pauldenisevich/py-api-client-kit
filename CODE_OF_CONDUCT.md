# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful and constructive experience for everyone.

This project should remain focused on building useful, reliable, well-tested open-source software.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive project environment include:

* using respectful and professional language
* giving and accepting constructive feedback
* staying focused on the technical topic
* being clear about assumptions and tradeoffs
* respecting different experience levels
* helping keep discussions actionable and relevant

## Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

* harassment, threats, or personal attacks
* insulting, demeaning, or discriminatory comments
* publishing another person's private information
* spam or intentionally disruptive behavior
* repeated off-topic arguments after maintainers ask to stop
* sharing secrets, credentials, private keys, tokens, or sensitive data in public project spaces

## Maintainer Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying expectations and may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that do not align with this code of conduct.

Maintainers may temporarily or permanently restrict participation for behavior that is harmful to the project or its community.

## Reporting

If you experience or observe unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainer privately.

Do not include secrets, credentials, private keys, tokens, or sensitive customer data in public reports.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, documentation, examples, and other public project communication channels.
